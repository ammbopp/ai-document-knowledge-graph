In 2023, ABC Tech collaborated with SmartFarm Solutions to develop an intelligent
agricultural analytics platform. The project aimed to improve crop yield prediction
and resource management for large-scale farms.

The platform uses Machine Learning and IoT sensors to collect real-time data
from soil, weather stations, and water systems. The collected data is processed
using cloud-based analytics services.

John Miller was appointed as the project manager and worked closely with
researchers from GreenField University. The research team focused on building
predictive models for water usage and disease detection.

The project started in 2023 and was officially deployed in Chonburi, Thailand in 2024.
Funding for the project was provided by the National Innovation Agency.

After deployment, the system helped farmers reduce water consumption by 20 percent
and increased crop yield by 15 percent. ABC Tech plans to expand the platform
to other regions in Southeast Asia.
